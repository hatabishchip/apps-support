Support

We read every message and reply within two business days.

Email us

Write to support@detme.com for anything: an account problem, a question about how matching works, a bug, a refund, or feedback.

To help us answer faster, tell us the email you signed up with and, if something went wrong, what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Why do I only see a few people a day? That is deliberate. DetMe picks a small set of compatible matches once a day instead of an endless feed. A new set arrives the next day.

I am a man and cannot browse women. That is by design, not a bug. Women are never browsed on DetMe. A woman sees you only if you fit what she is looking for, and she decides.

I want to delete my account. You can do it yourself inside the app: Profile, then Delete account. Everything is removed. See Account deletion.

Someone is behaving badly. Use Report or Block on their profile or inside the chat. Reports go to our moderators. For anything involving a minor, see Child safety and write to us directly.

Report a safety concern

For urgent safety matters, including child sexual abuse and exploitation, email support@detme.com with SAFETY in the subject line. Those messages are handled first. Details of our standards are on the Child safety page.
